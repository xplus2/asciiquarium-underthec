macOS screensaver
=================

By default, downloaded binaries are quarantined.
Clear that before first use:

  xattr -d -r com.apple.quarantine underthec.saver


Double-click `underthec.saver`, or copy it to `~/Library/Screen Savers` (single-user)
or to `/Library/Screen Savers` (global).

Then select "Under The C" in System Settings -> Screen Saver and enjoy the mysteries of the C.
